# Thank you and credits

## Thank you

AboutCode exists thanks to the contributions of over 700 open source developers and
contributors. We are grateful for the time, effort, and expertise they bring to our
shared mission of making software supply chain data open and accessible.

See the contributors to our key projects:

- [ScanCode toolkit contributors](https://github.com/aboutcode-org/scancode-toolkit/graphs/contributors)
- [VulnerableCode contributors](https://github.com/aboutcode-org/vulnerablecode/graphs/contributors)
- [ScanCode.io contributors](https://github.com/aboutcode-org/scancode.io/graphs/contributors)
- [PurlDB contributors](https://github.com/aboutcode-org/purldb/graphs/contributors)

We also depend on and are thankful for major open source projects including:

- [Python](https://www.python.org) is the primary language of most AboutCode tools
- [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org) is our database of choice
- [Django](https://www.djangoproject.com) is the web framework powering our applications
- [Redis](https://redis.io) provides task queue and caching
- [Docusaurus](https://docusaurus.io) powers this website
- [React](https://reactjs.org) is the UI framework used here
- [nginx](https://nginx.org) serves our deployments
